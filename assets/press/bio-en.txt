MAKHOOLA

Makhoola is a band from Rasht, Iran, active since 1392 in the Iranian calendar (2013–14). The name comes from Nima Yushij's poem "Makh Oola", about a long river that "flows on, unknown".

Broadly alternative rock, the band describes itself as a meeting of many different tastes, so its songs can differ widely from one another. Most of them set Persian poetry to music: Nima Yushij, Ahmad Shamlou, Forough Farrokhzad, Fereydoon Moshiri, Baba Taher and Golchin Gilani, and sometimes the members' own words.

Their debut album, "Khoroos" (The Rooster), was released in March 2021 by Raha Records. Their second album, "Nemikaham", is coming soon.
